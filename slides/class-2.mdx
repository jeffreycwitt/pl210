made and governs the world) is by the art of man, as in many other

rational and most excellent work of nature, man. For by art is created
that great LEVIATHAN called a COMMONWEALTH, or STATE…”

Question: Why is this a radical statement?

Question: If the State is an invention of human art (and does not and
did not exist by nature) what further question does this raise?
