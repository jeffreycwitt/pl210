(Note that while the idea of a religious government is very foreign to us in the present, it was a central issue in the English Civil War. Both sides appealed to God as the ultimate political authority and the justification for their claims to power. In keeping with Hobbes's proclivity to break with the past, his aim here is to show that a religious government will not, in the end, work.)
