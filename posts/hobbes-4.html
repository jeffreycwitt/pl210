Leviathan, c. 20-21

How does it relate to fear?
How does it relate to necessity?
What is the relation of civil laws to liberty?
What metaphor does he use to describe these laws?

Why are people often deceived about liberty? Why does he think people like Cicero and Aristotle have gone wrong?
What kinds of things can the Sovereign not make laws about? What rights are left to the individual and why?
